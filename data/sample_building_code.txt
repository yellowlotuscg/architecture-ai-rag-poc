# Synthetic Building Code Excerpt

## 1001.2 Accessible Route
### Clear width
An accessible route serving a dwelling entry shall maintain a clear width of not less than 36 inches. Doors in the route shall provide at least 32 inches of clear opening.

### Exception
Where an existing wall condition prevents the required clear width, an alteration may use 32 inches of clear width when the authority having jurisdiction approves the documented constraint.

## 1002.4 Bathroom Turning Space
### Turning diameter
A bathroom serving an accessible dwelling unit shall provide a clear turning space with a 60 inch diameter, unless the approved design uses a compliant T-shaped turning space.

### Fixture clearance table
Lavatory front clearance: 30 inches minimum.
Water closet side clearance: 16 inches minimum from centerline to side wall.

## 1003.1 Bedroom Egress
### Emergency escape
Each sleeping room shall have an operable emergency escape and rescue opening with a minimum clear opening of 5.7 square feet, subject to local amendments and approved alternate provisions.
